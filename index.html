# about

no fluff, what I actually do

I lean product and systems over pure development. Given a problem, I'd rather work out how the whole thing should run and drive the process to ship it, then write the specific code that's actually missing, not the other way round.

Discord is the clearest example of this in practice. I've put real time into the infrastructure side of it: bots, permissions, moderation, anti-raid setup, not just sitting in servers as a member.

Longer term, I'm working toward AI systems for autonomous surveillance and drone platforms. The tools and ops work here are groundwork for that, even on the days it doesn't look like it.
